How it works

Three steps, and everything is on record.

LogHand overFind

01 · Log

At the front desk, nothing gets lost from the first minute.

  • Pick the file type: the list of required documents appears and you tick the ones handed in.
  • The file gets a unique reference to write on the paper folder.
  • The client leaves with an acknowledgement of receipt: a QR code to track the file and the list of what is still to bring.

02 · Hand over

A signed handover in ten seconds, instead of a register.

  • The file changes hands: enter who receives it and in which office. The recipient is notified by email.
  • They sign on screen with a finger. The handover receipt is created, dated and ready to print.
  • The handover log answers the awkward question: who had it, and since when?

03 · Find

Your clients track their file without calling you.

  • The client scans the QR code on their acknowledgement: received, in progress or completed, they see it on their phone.
  • If documents are missing, the list is right there. They know what to bring without coming in to ask.
  • They see only what matters: your internal notes and exchanges stay with you.

Meanwhile · Follow up

Pending files no longer gather dust.

  • Every file waiting on a document from the client is gathered on one page.
  • One click prepares the reminder email with the exact list of what is missing.
  • Each reminder is dated in the history: you know who was reminded, and when.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us before they start.

Do we have to give up paper?

No. Files stay in their folders. Suivix adds a QR label and keeps a record of every move: paper and screen finally tell the same story.

What happens to the files we already have in progress?

You import your Excel register in one go. Each file gets its reference and current holder, then you stick the QR labels on the folders.

Our team is not comfortable with computers.

Day-to-day use comes down to three screens: log, hand over, find. Training is done by role (front desk, team members, department heads, management) and one person on your side is trained as the in-house champion.

How long before we are up and running?

Two weeks as a rule: the account and team on day one, files in progress during the first week, normal rhythm in the second.

How do we pay?

By Orange Money, MTN MoMo or bank transfer, every month. The Discovery plan is free with no commitment: you can test with a single department before deciding.
